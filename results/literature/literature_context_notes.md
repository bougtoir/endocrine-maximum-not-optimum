# Literature context notes — JOE literature-reinforcement pass

Purpose: document the verified context for each reference added or repositioned in the
revised manuscript. No claim in the manuscript rests on an unverified or search-snippet-only source.

## A. Glucose–insulin–glucagon modelling lineage
- **Bergman et al. 1979** (minimal model) + **Bergman 2021** (its history) — establish that a few
  states quantify insulin sensitivity from IVGTT; motivates the "quantitative model" tradition.
- **Sorensen 1985** (thesis) — origin of the whole-body lineage; previously listed but uncited
  (orphan). Now cited where the lineage is discussed and implicitly via Panunzi.
- **Panunzi et al. 2020** — the revised Sorensen model used here (validated, CC-BY).
- **Dalla Man et al. 2014** — modern glucagon-aware simulator: includes glucagon secretion
  and action in the hypoglycaemic range; shows whole-system feedback modelling is mainstream.

## B. Hypoglycaemic counter-regulation
- **Schwartz et al. 1987** — stepped-clamp glycaemic thresholds: glucagon ~68, epinephrine ~69 mg/dL,
  symptoms ~53 mg/dL. Anchors the physiological-plausibility argument: stronger insulin action buys
  clearance at the price of entering the counter-regulatory zone.

## C. α/β-cell cross-talk (beyond Garzilli & Itzkovitz)
- **Kawamori et al. 2009** — α-cell insulin receptor knockout → hyperglucagonemia: in-vivo evidence
  for β→α (insulin suppresses glucagon).
- **Capozzi et al. 2019** — proglucagon peptides define β-cell tone via cAMP: α→β stimulation of
  insulin secretion. Together with [3] these make the reciprocal-loop claim three-source strong.

## D. Homeostasis / control theory
- **Cannon 1929** — homeostasis as organised whole-system regulation (conceptual frame).
- **Kitano 2004** — biological robustness: stronger local action can recruit counter-regulation.
- **Wang 2012** — optimality of homeostatic control depends on operating point (bistable regimes);
  used for context-dependence of u* in Discussion (kept, recited).

## E. Non-monotonic pharmacology — the distinction, not the example
- **Calabrese & Baldwin 2001** (hormesis) + **Vandenberg et al. 2012** (endocrine NMDR) —
  both describe target-level non-monotonic dose–response to exogenous exposure. The manuscript
  now states explicitly how the present phenomenon differs: the intervention u is monotonic by
  construction; the U-shape emerges at system level through feedback.

## F. IVGTT/FSIGT benchmark (limitation, honest wording)
- **Thomaseth et al. 2014** — IM-IVGTT induces transient hypoglycaemia only in insulin-sensitive
  subjects and engages counter-regulation; used to word the limitation precisely
  ("exceeds the transient, subject-dependent counter-regulatory dips described for insulin-modified
  FSIGT protocols") rather than inventing a clinical range.

## G. Quantitative HPT feedback models
- **Pandiyan, Merrill & Benvenga 2014** — patient-specific HPT negative-feedback model;
  cited to mark our HPT extension as exploratory relative to richer existing models.

- Onishi Array 2026, Onishi CSF 2026 — listed but never cited in the main text (orphans);
  removed to satisfy the every-entry-must-be-cited rule. No scientific content lost.
