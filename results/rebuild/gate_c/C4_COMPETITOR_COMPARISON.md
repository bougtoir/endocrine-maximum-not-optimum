# C4 — Comparison with the simplest competing explanations

| finding | simplest existing explanation | does the Sorensen-model result add anything? |
|---|---|---|
| (a) interior optimum of secretory gain | weighted hypo/hyper risk applied to a monotone dose–response; delayed negative feedback | it puts numbers on the trade-off for one model and protocol; no new mechanism |
| (a′) delay-dependent critical gain | gain margin of a delayed feedback loop | no; and it is conditional on the protocol |
| (b) interior optimum of endogenous-secretion inhibition under CIVII | exogenous insulin excess plus fixed 70/140 thresholds | no |
| (c) interior optimum of glucagon suppression | run-in artifact plus reference-dependent endpoints | no (artifact) |

This model has no incretin or mixed-meal mechanisms, so nothing here generalises to mixed meals. It also has no T1DM, T2DM or HPT parameterisation, so no disease generalisation is made.
