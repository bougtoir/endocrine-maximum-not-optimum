# C4 — Testable predictions (no composite score required)

None of these is a clinical dosing or treatment recommendation.

**P-1. From case (a′).** Under a 100 g glucose load starting from a low-normal fasting glucose, the gain at which the nadir reaches 70 mg/dL falls as insulin action is delayed.
- Model values: it falls from about 0.69 to 0.30 when the effective insulin-action time constant goes from 12.5 to 50 min.
- Possible test: hyperglycaemic or OGTT studies that measure insulin secretory rate (C-peptide deconvolution) and the hepatic insulin-action delay (tracer clamp).
- Expected outcome: the prediction is generic to delayed feedback. A positive result would confirm a textbook principle, not a model-specific mechanism. It is also not robust in the model at 50/75 g or at a basal glucose of 90 mg/dL.

**P-2. From case (b).** During a 0.25 mU/kg/min insulin infusion, the endogenous insulin contribution (C-peptide) falls. Pharmacological suppression of that residual contribution should lower the plateau glucose less than proportionally, because glucagon counterregulation compensates.
- The model predicts that clamping counterregulation removes the interior optimum (A1/A2 → Type I).
- Testable with somatostatin plus glucagon-replacement designs, which already exist in the literature. Not novel.
